Mouse recordings from keyboardsounds-pro by Nathan Fiscaletti,
https://github.com/keyboard-sounds/keyboardsounds-pro (desktop/bundled-profiles at commit
bac56ac700635c512e57621f35780c5b79eba4cd), MIT licence: see LICENSE. Its profiles credit
MechVibesPlusPlus (https://github.com/PyroCalzone/MechVibesPlusPlus, MIT: see
LICENSE-MechVibesPlusPlus) for all packs except g502x-wireless.

scroll-down and scroll-up are the wheel button being pressed and released (the middle
click), not scroll ticks. The g502x-wireless files were renamed to match the others:
primary_* -> left-*, secondary_* -> right-*, middle -> scroll-down.

All files are stored as 16-bit mono WAV: those that were MP3 or stereo were converted.
